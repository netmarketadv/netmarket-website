# Homepage Strategy

Date: 2026-09-02

Purpose: define what the homepage must prove before visual or copy changes are made.

## Job Of The Homepage

The homepage should answer five user questions quickly:

1. Who is Netmarket?
2. What does Netmarket actually do?
3. Why should I trust them?
4. Have they solved problems like mine?
5. What is the next step?

## Recommended Structure

| Section | Role | Data source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Positioning and conversion | Static copy until CMS global copy exists | Lead with integrated communication, web, marketing and technology. |
| Trust strip | Immediate credibility | CMS/client data when available | Client logos and Google rating must be real. |
| Service system preview | Breadth and orientation | CMS services when populated, fallback until then | Avoid 9 identical cards. Use editorial rows or compact grouped modules. |
| Selected projects | Proof | CMS case studies required for final | Do not invent metrics or case-study claims. |
| Method | How Netmarket works | CMS/static documented process | Keep clear and concrete, not agency jargon. |
| Team/agency proof | Entity trust | CMS people/company data | Use real photos and roles only. |
| Insights | Editorial depth | CMS posts | Link to real insight URLs, not contact anchors. |
| Reviews/testimonials | Trust | Google Places/CMS cache | Show real reviewer data where API/license allows. |
| Final CTA | Conversion | Static | Prefer simple CTA: `Parliamone.` |

## Current Gaps

- Homepage still relies heavily on local fallback/static data.
- Case studies, clients, people, resources and testimonials are not yet available from CMS cache.
- Insight cards on homepage should link to real insight pages where present.
- Claims must remain conservative until project metrics and testimonial sources are populated.

## Copy Direction

Use short concrete statements. Prefer:

- `Comunichiamo valore.`
- `Web, marketing, comunicazione e tecnologia nello stesso sistema.`
- `Dal 1986 aiutiamo aziende a progettare, comunicare e crescere con strumenti digitali concreti.`

Avoid:

- generic claims like `soluzioni digitali innovative`;
- keyword stuffing around `Padova`;
- unverifiable performance promises;
- slogans that do not explain what Netmarket does.
